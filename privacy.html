Privacy Policy

NobleVeyra Advisory respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you visit our website, contact us, book a service, or purchase advisory services, and how that information may be used.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with NobleVeyra Advisory, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and other contact information you voluntarily provide.
  • Information you submit through questionnaires, forms, email, or booking communications about your business, goals, challenges, and advisory needs.
  • Transaction-related information associated with a purchase. Full payment card information is handled by third-party payment processors and is not stored directly by NobleVeyra Advisory.
  • Basic website usage information, such as browser type, device type, referring pages, and similar technical information when analytics or website tools are enabled.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information you provide to:

  • Deliver Business Launch Strategy Sessions, 30-Day Business Launch Intensive services, and related advisory support.
  • Communicate with you about bookings, payments, questionnaires, scheduling, service delivery, and follow-up.
  • Respond to questions and determine which NobleVeyra service may be appropriate for your situation.
  • Improve our services, website, client experience, and business operations.
  • Maintain records for accounting, legal, fraud-prevention, and administrative purposes.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Stripe or another processor displayed at checkout. NobleVeyra Advisory does not directly store full payment card numbers. Your payment information is subject to the privacy and security practices of the payment processor you use.

4. Scheduling and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools for payment processing, scheduling, email, website hosting, forms, analytics, and related business functions. Information may be shared with those providers only as reasonably necessary to provide or operate those services.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies needed for website functionality. If analytics tools are enabled, they may collect limited technical information about how visitors use the site. You may be able to limit cookies through your browser settings.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the business, when required by law, to protect our legal rights, or with your consent.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, client support, business records, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and legitimate operational purposes.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information under our control. No method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update or correct personal information you previously provided. Where applicable, you may also request deletion of information that we are not required to retain for legal, financial, contractual, or legitimate business purposes.

10. Children

NobleVeyra Advisory is intended for adults seeking business advisory services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest published version.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to hello@nobleveyraadvisory.com.
